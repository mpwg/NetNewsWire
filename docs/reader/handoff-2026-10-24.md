- FreshRSS is the sole account provider; no migration of legacy NetNewsWire databases.
- Bundle IDs `eu.mpwg.reader` and Debug `eu.mpwg.reader.debug`; separate stores and Keychain services.
- Per-feed ordinary articles or daily digests, with originals always accessible.
- On-device Apple inference by default. PCC stays disabled until Apple entitlement/provisioning and explicit per-feed consent are verified.
- Full text is extracted locally when needed; intelligence preferences and digests stay device-local.
- Opening content changes no status. Marking a displayed digest read affects only that revision's sources.
- Additional providers, direct RSS/Atom ingestion, widgets, extensions, AppleScript and theme import are outside version one.
