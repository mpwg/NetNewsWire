# Known evening-weekday omission — 2026-10-27

The earlier actual local-model news output generalized a Thursday-only evening opening by omitting Thursday. The source and generated text are synthetic and are preserved in [the original fixed model report](i-key-statements-model-series-2026-10-26.json); this is the documented weekday error that the separate model reviewer accepted in three of three observations. The owner chose free app-language summaries and key-statement selection, while requiring conditions of selected claims to remain intact.

A conservative English/German rule now examines source and generated sentences for recognized weekday names with `evening`/`Abend`. If a referenced source sentence binds an evening claim to a recognized weekday, an evening sentence in the summary must contain one of those recognized source weekdays. The check runs at native-generator completion, independently before coordinator publication and before cache reuse. A rejected new result follows the existing warning/originals-only path; a rejected cached result is regenerated. Source references, article/read state and outbox remain intact. No prompt or data migration changes.

This is a narrow lexical rejection, not semantic entailment. It does not understand every time expression, negation, language, source-claim relationship or unrelated statement. If several referenced sources or sentences discuss different evening claims, a matching weekday alone does not establish that the particular claim is supported. An unrelated evening sentence or an implicit weekday carried across sentences can cause conservative abstention. If no matching source weekday or evening expression is recognized, this check alone cannot establish correctness. The full summary and offer-quality criteria remain open.
