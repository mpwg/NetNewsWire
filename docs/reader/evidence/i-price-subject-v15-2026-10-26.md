Status: **research candidate; complete Mac code selection passes, actual public-model assessment and full acceptance pending**. Main remains v10. The owner chose free app-language summaries plus source-bound offer facts and requires incomplete conditions to be visible as such.

The model names the source phrase for the item an amount pays for as part of primary offer extraction. Both the price and this subject are independently resolved through the role catalog and verified against the fragment and original. Reader retains a displayed price only if that source phrase agrees with the model's selected product name, using case-insensitive literal comparison. If a subject is missing or differs, Reader withholds the price and its subject; the existing price field displays “See original”, while the selected product, other sourced terms, free summary and links can remain available. It does not rewrite a source value or turn the qualifying cost into a different price.

This is a conservative name-consistency check, not semantic entailment: the model might name the wrong product and price subject consistently, or two legitimate names for one item might differ. It also does not prove completeness of conditions or the accuracy of free prose. The candidate's typed offer stores the price subject as an optional source quote; cache reuse validates it. Older typed snapshots decode without the field but a priced offer without a subject is invalid for reuse. Existing v7/v10 snapshots without typed offers retain their decoding path, and the changed v15 fingerprint regenerates older caches without changing original/read/outbox state.
